OrderDesk privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

OrderDesk is an ordering app for businesses: shop owners order from the distributors they choose to join, and distributors (with their staff and field agents) manage products, stock and orders. This page explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

DataWhy
Mobile numberTo sign you in with a one-time code sent on WhatsApp, and so distributors can call you about an order.
Your name, shop name, owner name, address, city, pincodeTo deliver orders and print them on bills.
Shop location (map pin) – only if you tap “Use my current location”So the delivery person can find the shop. It is optional and taken only while you use that button; the app never tracks location in the background.
Orders and cart (products, quantities, prices, notes, payment status)To run the ordering service and show reports to the distributor.
Photos that distributors upload (logos, products, banners, categories)To show the catalogue. Shops do not upload photos.
A notification token for your phoneTo send order updates (for example “order approved” or “new order”).

We do not show ads, do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and do not sell or rent any data.

Who can see it

  • A distributor (its owner, staff and assigned agents) sees the shop details and orders of shops that joined that distributor. A distributor never sees your orders with other distributors.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Meta (WhatsApp, to deliver the sign-in code), Expo and Google Firebase (to deliver notifications), and our cloud host (servers in India). They process data only to provide these services.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your account data stays while your account is active. Orders are business records of the distributor they were placed with, so a copy of each order (with the shop name and address printed on its bill) stays with that distributor even after you delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the app: open Profile (or More) and tap Delete my account. Without the app: use the account deletion page. Deleting logs you out everywhere and removes your name, mobile number, shop details and location, your links to distributors, your cart and notification tokens, and any team role you have. The only owner of a distributor must contact us to close the distributor account first.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in codes and session tokens are stored only as secure hashes.

Children

OrderDesk is for businesses and is not meant for children under 18.
